Made for review.

This preview is separate from orynb.com. Live replacement requires your approval.

What is implemented

Native HTML hero text and navigation; separate cinematic environment and product imagery; responsive layouts; two genuine WebGL scenes; original five-view product galleries; image zoom and keyboard navigation; Daily System bundle; persistent local launch bag; touch scratch-reveal with an accessible button; validated consent form; reduced-motion and WebGL fallbacks.

Motion fidelity limitation

The bottle and jar are real 3D meshes modeled from the supplied photos, with physically based materials. The original photographed label ink is used as the curved front texture. The cap twists and lifts; 3D capsules expand, suspend in depth, and return before the cap closes. OUTPUT has a separate orbital particle treatment.

No manufacturer CAD, measured packaging dimensions, original label dieline, or approved rendered sequence was supplied. Geometry and capsule behavior are art-directed approximations. Back-label fidelity is unverified. This is not yet a manufacturer-exact, approved final product render. Exact fidelity requires proper source assets and a final animation review.

What remains before live release

  • Approved pricing, shipping, taxes, stock and selling arrangements if checkout is to open.

  • Real-device mobile GPU/touch checks beyond browser emulation. A comparison against an approved rendered sequence needs that source asset.

Preview behavior

The launch bag is a local selection aid. No prices are invented and no payments are accepted. The founders’ reveal gives everyone the same invitation without promising a discount. The form validates input but clearly reports that registration is not yet connected.
