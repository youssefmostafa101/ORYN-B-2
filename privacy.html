Overview

ORYN B respects your privacy. This page should explain what personal information the store collects, why it is collected, how it is used, and the choices available to customers.

Information we collect

When the commerce system is connected, it may collect information needed to provide the store and fulfill orders, such as contact details, shipping information, order details, communications, device information, and site activity. Payment information should be processed by the selected payment provider rather than stored directly by this static site.

How information is used

Information may be used to operate the store, fulfill orders, respond to customer requests, maintain site security, comply with applicable obligations, and—where permitted—to send communications a customer has asked to receive.

Providers and analytics

Before enabling Shopify, Stripe, analytics, email, advertising, or embedded services, update this policy to name the services, describe their data practices, and provide any required consent controls.

Your choices

Customers may have rights to access, correct, delete, or opt out of certain uses of personal information, depending on where they live. Publish a working contact method and the required regional disclosures before launch.

Updates

ORYN B may update this policy when its practices change. Replace this draft with an approved policy and effective date before collecting personal information.
