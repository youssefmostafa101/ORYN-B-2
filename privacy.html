ORYN B / Launch list

Your email. Your choice.

This page describes the current coming-soon website and its launch signup form.

What the form saves

If you choose to join, the website saves your email address, the formula you selected (or both), the consent wording and version, and the time of signup. This is used to manage ORYN B launch news and marketing updates.

Where it goes

Signup records are stored in the website’s server database on Cloudflare through Sites hosting. Omnisend is not connected yet. No automated email is sent by this form. Before launch messages are sent, the email service and its unsubscribe process will be configured and tested.

Changing your mind

After a successful signup, a “Remove my signup” button lets you delete your record while the confirmation remains open. The removal key stays only in the page’s memory and is cleared when you leave or reload. Future marketing emails will include an unsubscribe option.

Cookies and tracking

The website application uses no advertising trackers, third-party fonts or tracking cookies. It does not save your email in browser storage. Hosting may process network requests and access authentication. To limit abuse, the signup service keeps a short-lived hashed network identifier and a request count; expired counters are removed during subsequent signups.

Prelaunch status

There is no checkout, payment processing or purchase on this site. Signup records are kept for the launch list until removed or the list is closed. The business privacy notice and contact process will be finalized before the website opens publicly at orynb.com.
